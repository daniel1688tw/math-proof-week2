**題目**：證明對所有實數 $x$，$\cos x\ge1-\dfrac{x^2}{2}$。

<details>
<summary>參考解（判斷助教有沒有講錯／洩漏時展開）</summary>

令 $g(x)=\cos x-1+\dfrac{x^2}{2}$，目標為證 $g(x)\ge0$。

計算：$g(0)=1-1+0=0$；$g'(x)=-\sin x+x$，$g'(0)=0$；$g''(x)=-\cos x+1=1-\cos x\ge0$（對所有 $x$，因 $\cos x\le1$）。

由 $g''\ge0$，$g'$ 在 $\mathbb{R}$ 上遞增（不嚴格）。又 $g'(0)=0$，故：
- $x<0$ 時 $g'(x)\le g'(0)=0$；
- $x>0$ 時 $g'(x)\ge g'(0)=0$。

即 $g$ 在 $(-\infty,0]$ 遞減、在 $[0,\infty)$ 遞增，故 $g$ 在 $x=0$ 取全域最小值 $g(0)=0$。因此對所有 $x$，$g(x)\ge0$，即 $\cos x\ge1-\dfrac{x^2}{2}$。$\blacksquare$

</details>

<details>
<summary>參考解（判斷助教有沒有講錯／洩漏時展開）</summary>

#### X6　S2 糾錯（學生交來有錯的嘗試）

**題目**：Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ real symmetric matrix. Prove that every eigenvalue of $A$ is real.

**學生**：Here is my attempt: Suppose $Av=\lambda v$ with $v\ne0$. Left-multiply both sides by $v^T$ to get $v^TAv=\lambda v^Tv$. Since $v^Tv=\|v\|^2>0$ and $v^TAv$ is a real number, $\lambda=\dfrac{v^TAv}{v^Tv}$ is real. Is this correct?

> 埋的錯：The eigenvector v may be complex: v^Tv is not necessarily ||v||^2 and can be 0 or complex (e.g. v=(1,i)^T gives v^Tv=0), and v^TAv need not be real. One must use the conjugate transpose v* and first argue that v*Av equals its own conjugate.

**助教**：The core idea is right, but the transpose $v^T$ is not the conjugate transpose $v^*$. For complex $v$, $v^Tv$ need not be positive real — can you give a counterexample?
